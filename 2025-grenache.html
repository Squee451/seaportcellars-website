2025 Grenache

Los Rocosos Vineyard · The Rocks District of Milton-Freewater

Same Grape. A Whole Different Story.

For our second vintage, we've traveled from the elevated vineyards of the Rogue Valley to one of the Pacific Northwest's most distinctive winegrowing regions: The Rocks District of Milton-Freewater.

At Los Rocosos Vineyard, vines grow among the remarkable cobblestones that define this landscape. It's a dramatically different setting from the one that produced our inaugural Grenache—and the wine is proving just as different.

Co-fermented with 17% Syrah, our 2025 Grenache is darker, deeper, and more structured than its predecessor. Black cherry, savory spice, and firm, polished tannins suggest a wine with both personality and the potential to develop beautifully.

Where the 2024 emphasizes elegance and immediate pleasure, the 2025 explores Grenache's more powerful, savory side.

At the Table

Think grilled lamb, roast duck, wild mushrooms, slow-cooked meats, and the robust flavors of Mediterranean cooking. A wine to share over a leisurely dinner—and perhaps to tuck away a few bottles for another year.

The People Behind the Vineyard

Part of the pleasure of making wine is getting to know the people and places behind the grapes. Los Rocosos is an especially memorable stop in our continuing exploration of Pacific Northwest Grenache.
