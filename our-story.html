Our Story

My love of wine began in an unlikely place: the circus.

During my time as a clown with Ringling Bros. and Barnum & Bailey, a few of us developed a Saturday evening ritual. Between shows, we'd climb to the highest seats in the arena, open a bottle of wine, and look down at the three rings far below. We'd talk about our long, strange journeys, wonder where life might take us next, and enjoy the moment. Wine had a way of making those conversations even better.

Over a lifetime of tasting wines, I've become particularly fascinated by Grenache. From the great Châteauneuf-du-Papes of the Southern Rhône to the steep terraces of Priorat and the Cannonau wines of Sardinia, I've been startled and delighted by how differently the same grape can express itself.

When I began exploring vineyards in the Pacific Northwest, I recognized echoes of those distant landscapes in our own rocky soils, hillsides, and varied elevations. I wondered what Grenache might become here.

And living in the Willamette Valley, surrounded by wonderful Pinot Noirs, I liked the idea of making something a little different—a wine that could both compare and contrast with the region's celebrated grape.

That's how Seaport Cellars began - a small, personal project, driven by curiosity and the pleasure of discovering what Grenache can become in the Pacific Northwest. Same grape. Different places. Different wines. That's what fascinates me.

After all these years, I still believe a good bottle of wine is best enjoyed with interesting people, stimulating conversations, and perhaps a good story.

— Marty Sherman
